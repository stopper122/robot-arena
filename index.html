Plugging in VEX V5 controllers

Plug the V5 controller into the computer with a USB data cable (close VEXcode first) and press any button so the browser notices it. It works turned on or off. Use Chrome or Edge. Xbox-style gamepads work too, and two players can share the keyboard: WASD + Space and arrows + Enter. F = full screen for the projector.
